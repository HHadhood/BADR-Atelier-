Process

Every stage should answer a different question.

BADR's process is designed around decisions: first understand the opportunity, then compare ideas, resolve architecture, build information, coordinate the disciplines and issue clear deliverables.

PrincipleDecision before production
Quality gateReview before next stage
01 / DISCOVER

What are we really designing?

Site, client ambition, user profile, development logic, project constraints, area targets, program and decision criteria are established.

02 / EXPLORE

Which idea deserves to continue?

Several meaningful options are compared through planning logic, massing, privacy, value, circulation and architectural identity.

03 / RESOLVE

Does the architecture work?

Plans, façades, sections, rooms, materials, structure interfaces, services and key dimensions are resolved to a coherent design.

04 / MODEL

Is the information structured?

The design becomes a controlled model with coordinated geometry, parameters, schedules and drawing views.

05 / COORDINATE

Can the disciplines coexist?

Structural and MEP interfaces are tested, issues are assigned and major constructability risks are surfaced before delivery.

06 / COMMUNICATE

Can everyone understand the same project?

Drawings, models, visuals, schedules and executive presentation material are issued so technical and non-technical stakeholders can make informed decisions.

Quality Review

A design review is not a final check. It is part of the design.

BADR's internal review questions focus on clarity, privacy, circulation, proportion, structural logic, service stacking, buildability, market differentiation and presentation quality.
