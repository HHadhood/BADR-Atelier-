## Usage Principle
The positioning title identifies BADR's role. The service descriptor explains the integrated disciplines. The brand line expresses the outcome. Avoid stacking all three in very small spaces; next to the logo use the positioning title + service descriptor, while the brand line is preferred in hero, footer, portfolio transitions and campaign material.
