Development Case Study / ADEC

Al Rehab Oasis

A residential development study in Jeddah exploring how villa count, premium arrival and lifestyle can work as one commercial story.

ClientADEC
LocationJeddah
Site14,400 m²
Development Path32 Villas
Typical Footprint15×10 m
The question was not simply: how do we place 32 villas?

The study compared masterplan directions with different commercial and experiential priorities.

The preferred narrative linked product strategy to architecture and experience.
