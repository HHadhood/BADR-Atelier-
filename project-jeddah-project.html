Jeddah Development Concepts

A strategic set of Jeddah development concepts exploring distinct identities such as Roshan Oasis, Palestine Gate and Jeddah Courtyard.

TEST THE IDENTITY BEFORE FIXING THE FORM
Previous ProjectNext Project
LocationJeddah, Saudi ArabiaTypologyDevelopment Concepts / Residential + Urban Identity
Design Narrative

Before fixing one answer, the site is tested through different identities to reveal which development story deserves to lead.

The file is treated as a portfolio of options: each concept gives a different answer to the same development question — what should this site become, and how should the first image in the market feel?

Multiple design narratives tested to translate land, market and identity into differentiated development directions.

Roshan Oasis conceptPalestine Gate conceptJeddah Courtyard conceptArrival identityUrban frontageLandscape-led living
Three Development Directions

One site. Three identities. Each tested as a different market proposition.

01

Roshan Oasis

A landscape-led urban oasis shaped by water, shaded promenades and a central social heart.

02

Palestine Gate

A gateway-led composition with a stronger ceremonial axis, civic presence and destination arrival.

03

Jeddah Courtyard

A courtyard-based urban fabric where climate, intimacy, mixed-use activity and walkability shape the identity.

Strategic Lens — Option Intelligence

How can multiple design directions test market identity before the project commits to one?

Development Question

Which proposition — Roshan Oasis, Palestine Gate or Jeddah Courtyard — best aligns place, product and perception?

Governing Idea

Use contrasting concept narratives to expose trade-offs in identity, arrival, landscape and positioning.

Architectural Response

Each option is treated as a different development proposition rather than a cosmetic façade alternative.

Project Signature

One site, multiple possible futures — compared before design becomes expensive to change.

Why It Matters

Optionality is not indecision; it is a disciplined way to make a sharper early development decision.
