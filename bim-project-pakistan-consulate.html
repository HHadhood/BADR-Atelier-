BIM Story

BIM Here Is Not a Software Layer. It Is Continuity from Decision to Asset.

The journey begins with information that structures design and coordination, extends into construction planning and progress control, and reaches an asset model prepared for handover and operations. The goal is continuity of decisions across stages.

Information flow helps teams see the relationship between architectural decisions and building systems.

03 • BUILD

Planning, progress tracking and quality review connect the model to construction reality.

04 • HANDOVER

A structured as-built model becomes an information record rather than ending at drawing production.

05 • OPERATE

Asset, maintenance and building-operation data extend information value beyond handover.
